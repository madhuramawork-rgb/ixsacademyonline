About Us

Introduction to IXS Academy

IXS Academy — the Industry Excellence Center — is a hands-on industrial automation training academy based in Kochi, Kerala. We bring corporate training, college partnerships, and our flagship industry-immersion program together under one roof, built around center-based PLC labs rather than slides and simulations alone.

Our faculty and advisory panel are practitioners first, so what's taught reflects what's actually happening on the industry floor. Learners move through a structured path — from foundational certification to company-integrated placement — with real industry partners involved throughout. We're registered under the Udyam (MSME) scheme and connected with organisations such as the ANZ Chamber of Commerce, reflecting our commitment to recognised training standards.

Why IXS?

Five reasons learners choose us

Every learner starts from a different place — some need the fundamentals, others are ready to build. That's why our five-point approach meets you where you are: center-focused PLC labs, real industry visits, guided internships and dedicated placement support, not just classroom theory. It's this hands-on, individually paced path that sets IXS apart from a typical training institute.

01
Backed by Industry Experts
Curriculum and mentorship shaped by practitioners actively working in the field.
02
Centre-Focused, Hands-On PLC Labs
Classrooms built around real PLC, SCADA and control-panel hardware, not simulations alone.
03
Industry Visits
Structured visits to live plants and facilities woven through the program.
04
Assisted Internships
Real, hands-on internship placements built into the course itself.
05
Placement Assistance & Hiring Network
Dedicated placement support backed by a growing network of hiring partners.
